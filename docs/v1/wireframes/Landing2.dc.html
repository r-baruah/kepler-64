A tiny universe that learned to play chess.

It knows no chess strategy. Pieces pull along the lines they can move, and it learned the rest by playing itself.

Runs in your browser 85,500 games of self-play Open source
The one law

Pull follows the moves.

A piece pulls only where it could move, and stops at the first piece in its way. This rook stops at its own pawn.

Learned, not told

It found the piece values by itself.

Every piece started with the same weight. After 60,000 games against itself, it had ranked them. A second run found the same.

Chess players say 3, 3, 5, 9. This universe found 2.6, 3.3, 4.4, 8.2.

The Law Ledger

Every law had to earn its place.

We switched each one off and let it play itself. Laws that helped stayed. The rest you can still play with.
