OPEN-SOURCE CHESS ENGINE · GEN 4

A tiny universe that learned to play chess.

Kepler-64 knows no chess advice. Its only judgement is one physical field: every piece pulls along the lines it can move, and the pull stops at the first piece in the way. It learned how strong each law is by playing itself.

IN YOUR BROWSER · 4 GENERATIONS · OPEN SOURCE
01 · THE ONE LAW

Pull follows the moves.

A rook pulls along its file and rank, a knight in its L-shapes, and the pull stops at the first piece in the way. This rook's pull reaches its own pawn and goes no further.

The field beats counting material alone by 330 Elo (engine against engine).

02 · LEARNED, NOT TOLD

It found the piece values by itself.

Every piece started with the same weight. After 60,000 games against itself it sorted them: queen, rook, bishop, knight. A second universe landed on the same values.
